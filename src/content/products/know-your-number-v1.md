# Know Your Number

## The Four Buckets Money & Future Planning System

Money gets complicated quickly when every question is treated separately.

How much can we save?

How much should stay available for emergencies?

How much can we use for protection?

How much can we put toward the future?

How much can we spend and still enjoy our life?

The purpose of Know Your Number is to put those questions in the right order.

Before deciding what product to buy, what account to open, what debt to accelerate, or what long-term strategy to use, first determine what your household can realistically support.

Your number is the amount of monthly capacity that remains after the life you are already responsible for has been accounted for honestly.

The goal is not to squeeze every possible dollar out of your household.

The goal is to see clearly enough to make deliberate decisions.

By the end of this system, you will have:

1. a clear picture of monthly household income,
2. a realistic view of recurring living costs,
3. a defined amount for enjoyment and discretionary spending,
4. a list of current debt and financial obligations,
5. your true monthly margin,
6. an emergency-reserve target,
7. an optional educational family-protection estimate,
8. a set of future priorities,
9. and a one-page Money Plan you can actually use.

---

# Before You Begin

## This is a planning tool, not individualized financial advice

Know Your Number is educational.

It does not tell you that a particular insurance policy, investment, retirement account, annuity, debt strategy, tax strategy, or financial product is right for you.

Those decisions can depend on age, income, taxes, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, guarantees, fees, surrender periods, debt terms, family circumstances, insurance needs, existing assets, and many other factors.

Use this system to understand your household before making product decisions.

If you need individualized legal, tax, investment, insurance, or financial advice, work with an appropriately qualified professional.

## Keep sensitive information private

You do not need to record:

- Social Security numbers
- bank account numbers
- credit card numbers
- insurance policy numbers
- passwords
- login information
- exact locations of valuables
- exact locations of important documents
- security codes
- private access instructions

Use categories, totals, and descriptions instead.

This workbook is designed to help you make decisions without creating an unnecessary record of sensitive information.

# A Simple Monthly Reset

Your numbers will change.

Income changes.

Expenses change.

Children grow.

Debt disappears.

New responsibilities arrive.

Priorities change.

Once a month, review five questions:

1. What came in?
2. What went out?
3. What changed?
4. What deserves more attention now?
5. What is the next best use of our available margin?

The purpose of a financial system is not to predict every future decision.

It is to keep you close enough to reality that you can make the next decision well.

---

# Final Principle

Do not manufacture your budget around something another person wants you to buy.

Determine your actual capacity first.

Protect the life you are responsible for.

Enjoy the life you are living.

Build toward the life you hope to create.

And know your number before you commit your money.
