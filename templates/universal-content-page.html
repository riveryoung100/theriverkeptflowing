A note before reading

This page is educational and reflects general experience and understanding. Insurance policies, financial products, laws, eligibility, pricing, and coverage vary. Review your actual documents and consult an appropriately licensed professional before making a decision.

Begin with a strong opening paragraph. This should establish why the subject matters and what the reader will understand by the end.

The main body uses comfortable typography, controlled line length, meaningful spacing, and enough visual restraint for long-form reading. Paragraphs should remain conversational and clear.

The goal is not to sound complicated. The goal is to help the reader see the subject more clearly than they did before.

The first important principle

Use second-level headings for the main movements of the page. These headings should help someone understand the structure even when scanning.

A supporting distinction

Third-level headings divide a main section into smaller ideas without making the page feel fragmented.

A useful guide should create understanding rather than dependence.
