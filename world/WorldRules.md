# World Rules

These laws govern every design, content, geography, motion, and engineering decision in The River Kept Flowing.

1. The River Kept Flowing is a living reflective reality.
2. The world exists independently of the visitor.
3. The world is never unlocked; it is gradually recognized.
4. Visitors do not change pages; they continue their journey.
5. The River creates and connects the landscape.
6. The landscape is emotionally accurate before it is geographically exact.
7. The River does not become deeper; the visitor does.
8. Nothing exists only for aesthetics.
9. The world always extends beyond what can be seen.
10. Technology succeeds when it disappears.
11. Nature arrives before architecture.
12. Water always moves according to the land.
13. Every structure exists for a lived purpose.
14. Every bridge crosses something meaningful.
15. Every trail exists because it has been walked.
16. Every species belongs to its ecosystem.
17. Hospitality precedes commerce.
18. Mystery may deepen meaning but must never obstruct essential information.
19. Accessibility is part of hospitality, not an alternate experience.
20. The experience returns people to reality rather than helping them escape it.
