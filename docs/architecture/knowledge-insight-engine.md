# Knowledge Insight Engine

## Status

Phase 27D introduces deterministic knowledge insight generation.

The Insight Engine transforms deterministic reasoning into durable, reviewable knowledge insights.

## Pipeline

Knowledge Graph
    ↓
Reasoning Engine
    ↓
Insight Engine
    ↓
Evidence Validation
    ↓
Knowledge Insight

## Responsibilities

- Generate deterministic insights
- Preserve supporting evidence
- Calculate confidence
- Route review workflow
- Produce immutable insight records
- Maintain deterministic identifiers

## Future Scope

Future phases introduce:

- Insight clustering
- Cross-document synthesis
- Long-term trend detection
- Automatic hypothesis generation
- Human review workflows
