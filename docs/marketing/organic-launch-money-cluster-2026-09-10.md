## LinkedIn

A lot of financial advice starts with a percentage.

Save 10%.
Save 20%.
Follow a certain rule.

But two households earning the same amount can have completely different lives.

One may have childcare, medical costs, debt, or unpredictable income. Another may not.

So I think a better question is:

**How much can your household realistically save every month without constantly breaking the plan?**

I use four simple buckets:

**Live. Enjoy. Protect. Build.**

The goal is not to force your life into somebody else's percentage.

The goal is to understand what your life actually costs, protect what matters, and create a number you can repeat.

# Launch Post 3 — How Much Emergency Fund Should You Have?

## LinkedIn

Three months?

Six months?

A full year?

Those can all be useful reference points for an emergency fund.

But none of them tell you exactly what **your household** needs.

A better starting calculation is:

**Essential monthly expenses × desired months of protection = emergency-fund target**

Then adjust based on things like:

Income stability.
Number of earners.
Debt.
Children.
Insurance.
Vehicle age.
Medical exposure.
Job predictability.

The point is not to copy somebody else's number.

It is to build enough protection for the risks your household actually carries.
