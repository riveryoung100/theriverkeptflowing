## Outreach Rules

- Relevance before volume.
- No paid backlinks.
- No link exchanges.
- No mass automated outreach.
- Pitch one specific resource to one specific page.
- Explain the exact gap the article fills.
- Keep outreach concise and human.
- Track every contact and result.
