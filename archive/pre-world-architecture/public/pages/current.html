A living record of the present

This is where the river is flowing now.

I am building, learning, questioning, working, creating, preparing, and trying to become capable of carrying the life I believe is being placed before me.

Right now

I am building capacity for the life I am asking God to trust me with.

This season is not defined by one project, one occupation, or one ambition.

It is a season of preparation.

I am learning how to build systems that can continue working when motivation disappears. I am learning how to create value without sacrificing my family, my convictions, or the person I am becoming.

I am learning how to hold responsibility without allowing it to consume every part of my life.

Some days this looks like writing a page for this website between insurance calls. Some days it looks like studying policies, regulations, sales processes, marketing, Scripture, financial services, or the behavior of markets.

Some days it looks like walking the land with my son, cooking dinner for my family, stretching beside my desk, or carrying the trash half a mile while turning the walk into a workout.

The Current is where all of those things meet.

A snapshot of this season

What I am building

These are not separate lives. They are different parts of the same foundation.

01

The River Kept Flowing

I am building this website as a living autobiography, publication, teaching platform, business foundation, and record of a life still unfolding.

Every page is another piece of the story: where I came from, what I believe, what I am learning, what I am creating, and where the river may be going.

02

Insurance Mastery

I am learning everything I can about property and casualty insurance, insurable interests, underwriting, coverage limits, policy structure, agency protection, errors and omissions, and the systems required to serve people responsibly.

That includes auto, home, renters, landlord policies, personal umbrellas, boats, recreational vehicles, motorhomes, commercial lines, and life-insurance referrals.

03

Sales Systems

I am refining call structures for live leads, cold outreach, winbacks, cross-sales, voicemail returns, appointments, quotes, and policy binding.

I am studying how to create conversations that keep people attentive, reduce resistance, improve clarity, and guide a customer through a process without making the interaction feel forced.

04

Future Brokerages

I am preparing to build an independent property and casualty brokerage, a life-insurance brokerage, and a financial-services business.

I want the knowledge, licenses, systems, partnerships, and operational discipline to be established before I carry the full responsibility of ownership.

05

A Growing Body of Work

I am creating an ongoing video series about faith, stewardship, sales, leadership, philosophy, cooking, health, exercise, family, responsibility, and becoming.

I am also compiling a book of belief and philosophy: the principles, lessons, convictions, and ideas that align with the man I want to become.

06

Attraction Instead of Pursuit

I am beginning to study search-engine optimization, content creation, digital marketing, and organic lead generation.

The long-term goal is to build work that attracts the right people rather than depending forever on cold pursuit, interruption, and constant chasing.

07

Financial Capability

I am learning about life insurance, financial services, wealth strategies, market behavior, foreign exchange, charting, and trading.

One of my long-term ambitions is to become an eight-figure currency trader, but the deeper goal is mastery, independence, and the ability to choose how my time is spent.

08

A Stronger Body

My work currently keeps me seated for eight to ten hours at a time, so I am learning how to rebuild physical capacity inside the life I actually have.

Right now that includes stretching, squats beside the desk, walking the land, simple arm training, and preparing to return to boxing and kickboxing through heavy-bag work.

The reality of the season

I am doing something I promised myself I would never do again.

I am cold calling.

After years of door-to-door sales and outbound insurance work, I told myself that future opportunities would come to me. I believed I would never again spend my days chasing people who were not actively searching for what I offered.

I wanted inbound calls. Warm leads. Conversations with people who had already raised their hands.

But I have a second son on the way.

After leaving State Farm and stepping away from door-to-door sales, I knew I needed work that allowed me to remain physically present at home with my wife, my son, our animals, and the life I am trying to build.

So I accepted a position that requires an enormous amount of outbound activity.

On some days I make between 150 and 280 cold calls while also taking live leads, running quotes, making appointments, cross-selling policies, returning voicemails, and binding whatever business I can responsibly find.

I did not choose this season because it perfectly matched the life I wanted. I chose it because it allowed me to remain home while building that life.

The difference now is that the work no longer owns every empty space around it.

Between calls, I can work on this website. I can study. I can write. I can watch something that helps the day feel lighter. I can step outside. I can see my family. I can use the margins of employment to build the foundation of independence.

This season is teaching me that trust does not always mean being led immediately into ideal circumstances.

Sometimes trust means returning to difficult work with a different purpose.

Life between the work

The ordinary details matter too.

This platform is not intended to create the illusion of a perfectly ordered life.

Family

Most of my time outside work is spent with my son, walking, talking, playing, observing the land, and trying to remain present while another son prepares to enter our family.

I am learning that being physically near the people I love is not the same as being mentally available to them.

Food

I cook multiple times a day because my family needs to eat, but also because cooking is one of the ways I create, provide, experiment, and care for the people in front of me.

Movement

I am learning to stop waiting for a perfect training schedule. A set of squats beside the desk, a stretch between calls, or a half-mile walk can still become part of rebuilding my body.

Learning

My days are filled with insurance training, Scripture, sales processes, philosophy, marketing, finance, trading, writing, website development, and whatever else helps me become more capable.

Solitude

I am limiting access to my attention. I am allowing fewer voices into my life and creating distance from people, ideas, and environments that reinforce fear, distraction, or limiting beliefs.

Small Joys

Some afternoons include working on the website while watching Dragon Ball Super between calls.

I do not want this story to become so polished that it forgets the small things that made the days livable.

The life beyond survival

I am not only trying to earn more. I am trying to make room for life.

I want my family to reach a place where our choices are no longer dictated by poverty, lack, or constant urgency.

I want my wife to look at me and at the life we have built and feel relief.

I want us to be able to choose where we live, what we eat, where we go, and how we use our days without every decision being narrowed by scarcity.

I want clean water that feels nourishing instead of water that leaves residue on everything it touches.

I want access to fruit, vegetables, living soil, water, fish, wild food, and an environment capable of producing more than desert survival.

I want income to become a tool that protects time rather than a force that consumes it.

But I also know that freedom without purpose can become another kind of emptiness.

That is why I am asking these questions now.

When time finally belongs to me, I want to already know what deserves it.

Right now, God is teaching me

Capacity before increase.

God is teaching me to develop the capacity to hold the blessings He is actively using me to build.

He is teaching me to create an everlasting foundation in His name and for His glory.

He is teaching me how to outlast difficult seasons without abandoning the promise because the process became painful.

He is teaching me to rise above circumstances that attempt to plague what He has placed in front of me.

He is teaching me to transcend the boundaries of what I believed was possible.

And He is teaching me to remain faithful with the raw material, opportunities, resources, knowledge, time, relationships, and responsibility already in my hands.

The question is not only whether I can receive more. The question is whether I can become trustworthy enough to carry it.
