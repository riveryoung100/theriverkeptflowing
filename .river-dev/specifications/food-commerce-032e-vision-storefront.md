## 0. The unequivocal direction

Build **one exceptional, family-owned food company**. Every direct relationship formed through honest, consent-based local marketing should compound into an asset owned by the family. Customer acquisition and retention support this single company; unrelated ventures remain outside this workstream. Do not access or cross-market any other firm's customer data without a lawful, independent basis and consent.

The mission is **not** to be a permanently solo home cook. The long-term vision is a dedicated legally compliant food-production facility, perhaps with the founder living on-site/nearby if zoning, facility separation and rules permit; reliable staff, measured operations, and a transferable system. Later choices may include a flagship facility, company-owned locations, franchising, licensing, or selling the operating business. Franchising and company ownership are different models requiring different economics and legal frameworks. Expansion must be earned by proof of repeatable, compliant unit economics.

Our deliberate operating principle, inspired by Alex Hormozi's emphasis on focus, is **one company, one current bottleneck, one verified increment at a time**. Do not quote a supposed “10,000x every day” as a verified quantitative promise. Software, content, SEO, ads, storytelling, relationships, customer care, scheduling and reporting all serve The Table, not competing projects. ### Core ingredient philosophy

- Thoughtful, produce-forward, recognizable ingredients, deliciousness, value and consistent texture take precedence over slogan-based marketing.
- **Avoid ingredients containing the nine major U.S. food allergens in core formulations when practical:** milk, egg, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, sesame. This is a formulation preference, **not** an “allergen-free” claim. Verify supplier ingredient statements, shared equipment and cross-contact; some recipes may later have deliberately justified and conspicuously disclosed exceptions. Do not label unverified foods allergen-free or safe for every allergy.
- The founder may explore soy/tofu later for protein or creaminess, but it is **not in the current Southwest Bowl**. Concerns: soy allergen, moisture, freeze/reheat texture and profitability.
- “All natural” should not be adopted as an umbrella marketing claim until substantiation and applicable labeling rules are checked. Accurate individual-ingredient descriptions beat unverifiable health/wellness claims. No “low sodium” claim without validated nutrition values.
- Fresh or tropical fruit ripeness, fermentation and allergy are distinct safety issues. Do not claim ripe fruit or dilution eliminates allergens or safety risks; account for founder's concerns with process and ingredient verification.

### Fulfillment and economics

- Strategic intent: accept orders daily and eventually offer daily fulfillment **only within owner-set windows, batch lead times, capacity and legal constraints**. Avoid automatic promises of daily same-day service.
- Customer-facing goal: simple posted meals and **free scheduled pickup**, plus **free local delivery on qualifying baskets/service zones** where feasible. Include delivery economics in price models, with actual cost allocations for labor, mileage, fuel, insulated transport, packaging, delivery capacity, payment fees, taxes and waste. Do not assume either is actually free to perform. Do not settle pricing or tax treatment until evidence and qualified legal/tax analysis.
- Pickup choices: safe, staffed designated home/property pickup area (family privacy, road access, signage, handoff) and/or approved public meetup points or third-party property with permission. The user's land outside Big Spring means customers may drive 4–12 miles / 8–16 min; avoid imposing unnecessary trips on both sides. No unattended cooler for temperature-controlled foods. Any online-prepaid home or off-site pickup method must pass the exact Texas food-sale/delivery legal interpretation before activation; using another family member's address does NOT automatically solve this.
- Delivery: consented delivery address and scheduled arrival window; possible future delivery drivers. No customer-facing GPS coordinates, real-time map, route or location telemetry. Customer can see scheduled window, simple status and manual **“on the way” / “almost there”** updates; never fabricate status progression based on a timer.
- Secure tracking link per order, no exposure of others' orders or residential address without need. Operator taps to change status; append durable events with identity/timestamps, send optional opt-in email/SMS/site notifications with idempotency and safe retry. `I'm here` can be a manual customer signal for pickup. SMS integration requires consent/provider/cost review; do not configure or send real notifications absent authorization. Operator+customer pages must agree on state and must be tested with synthetic orders only.
- Eventually support scoped family/staff roles, team shifts, delivery assignment and fair, compliant compensation. Meals as benefits do not replace legal wages for employees.
